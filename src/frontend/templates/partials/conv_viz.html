Your image, reduced to a grid coarse enough to watch. The kernel carries its weights and eases from cell to cell; each stop multiplies the pixels under it by those weights and sums them into one output cell. All three colour channels, exactly as convolve2d does it — the working panel breaks one stop down into its terms. Coarse grids show the arithmetic but lose the picture; raise the grid slider until the photo reads, at the cost of the weights being too small to print on the kernel.
